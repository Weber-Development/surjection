---
title: Legal notes
description: What Surjection does and does not do from a legal perspective.
---

Surjection is a testing and documentation tool. It is **not legal advice**, and using it does not make a website or app compliant with WCAG, EN 301 549, the European Accessibility Act, the German BFSG, the Austrian BaFG, the Swiss BehiG or any other law.

**Surjection does**

- run the automated rules of axe-core and map them to WCAG and EN 301 549,
- list rules it could not decide as "needs manual review",
- generate a structured accessibility statement from the data you provide.

**Surjection does not**

- find all barriers. Automated tests cover only part of the success criteria,
- decide whether your service falls under a law, or which exemptions apply,
- review the content of the statement it generates.

**You decide** which pages and states to test, which findings to accept in a baseline, and what your statement says. Have accessibility and legal questions reviewed by a qualified person.
